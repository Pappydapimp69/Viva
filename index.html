    HOW TO PLAY

    CAMERA

    WASD / arrows pan · mouse wheel zoom · middle-drag pan · click the minimap to jump · Esc pause · Tab speed.

    ✊ PROTEST

    [1] March: left-click to send the selected bloc (Q switches bloc). The crowd routes itself around anything you block.
    [2] Casseurs: radicalise people under the cursor — they hunt parked cars. Fire spreads car to car on its own.
    [3] Fireworks: stun a police line.
    [4] Barricade: drag across a street.
    [5] Mobilise: call the group chat — more people pour in.
    Fire trucks: march your bloc (or send casseurs) onto a truck. Packed around it for a few seconds, the crew abandons it and it stops spraying until the crowd moves off.
    CROWD is how many people your movement draws: taking squares and burning cars grow it, gas and arrests shrink it; if it collapses you lose. ENERGY is what tools cost; it refills by itself and spending it never costs you people.

    🎮 CONTROLLER

    Left stick moves the camera; the reticle stays centred and snaps to squads, landmarks and fires · A does it (select a squad, place its line, march, gas…; hold A to draw a barricade) · B cancels · LB/RB change tool · Y next squad or bloc · X go to it · D-pad down jump to the hot spot · D-pad up Mobilise / Reinforce · right stick or triggers zoom · Start pause · Back speed. In menus: stick or D-pad to move, A to choose, B to go back.

    🛡 POLICE

    Click a squad (or 1–9), then right-click where it should form a line — it spans the street by itself.
    [C] Charge arrests casseurs · [G] Gas · [B] Barrier drag a line · [F] Pompiers click a fire · [R] Reinforce.
    Everything costs authority, which slowly regenerates.
